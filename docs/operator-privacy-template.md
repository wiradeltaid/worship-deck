# Congregation Privacy Notice Template (For Church Operators)

> **About this document:**
> WorshipDeck is self-hosted open-source software developed by Wira Delta Indonesia.
> Because the software is installed on a server managed by your own church or organization, Wira
> Delta Indonesia has no access to your installation or data.
>
> Under data protection legislation, including Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No.
> 27/2022) and international equivalents, **your church organization is the legal Data Controller**
> for the personal information stored in this application.
>
> This template is provided as a starting point to help church administrators inform their
> congregation members about how their data is used. Replace the bracketed placeholders (`[...]`)
> with your local congregation's specific details before distributing or publishing.

---

## English Template

### Privacy Notice for Congregation Members & Participants

**Organization / Church Name:** `[Name of Congregation / Church]`  
**Effective Date:** `[Date]`  
**Contact / Inquiries:** `[Email or Phone of Church Office / Secretariat]`

#### 1. Why We Collect and Use Your Information
`[Name of Church]` uses a church presentation system (WorshipDeck) to facilitate worship
services and church announcements. In connection with these activities, we process the following
information:
- **Member Names:** Displayed on service rundown sheets and projection slides for scheduled worship
  roles (e.g., song leaders, speakers, scripture readers, deacons).
- **Photographs:** Used on presentation slides to introduce service leaders, welcome new families,
  or highlight church activities and milestones.
- **Announcement Information:** Flyers and posters displaying service schedules, ministry updates,
  and church events.

#### 2. Where Your Data is Stored
All data collected for worship presentation is stored exclusively on a local computer or server
operated directly by `[Name of Church]`. No personal data, photographs, or service records are
uploaded to or accessible by third-party software vendors, cloud analytics services, or outside
companies.

#### 3. Photographs and Minor Protection
We respect the privacy of our members, especially children and youth:
- Photographs of children and youth will only be projected or displayed with prior consent from a
  parent or legal guardian.
- If you prefer that your photograph (or your child's photograph) not be displayed on the worship
  screens, please notify us and we will use an alternative slide layout with text only.

#### 4. Data Retention
Service schedules and uploaded media are retained on our local system for `[e.g., 1 year / as
needed for ministry archives]`. Outdated media and member records are periodically reviewed and
removed from our local system.

#### 5. Your Rights
Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:
- Inquire what information or photographs about you are currently stored in our presentation system.
- Request the immediate removal or replacement of your photograph or name from upcoming slide decks.
- Withdraw your consent for future photo usage at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact our church administration at `[Church Office Contact]`.

## Template Bahasa Indonesia

### Pemberitahuan Privasi untuk Anggota Jemaat & Pelayan Ibadah

**Nama Gereja / Organisasi:** `[Nama Jemaat / Gereja Lokal]`  
**Tanggal Berlaku:** `[Tanggal]`  
**Kontak Sekretariat / Pengurus:** `[Email / No. HP Sekretariat Gereja]`

#### 1. Tujuan Pengumpulan dan Penggunaan Data
`[Nama Gereja]` menggunakan sistem penampil ibadah (WorshipDeck) yang dikelola secara
mandiri (*self-hosted*) untuk mendukung kelancaran kebaktian dan pengumuman jemaat. Terkait kegiatan
tersebut, kami mengelola data berikut:
- **Nama Anggota / Pelayan:** Ditampilkan pada warta jemaat dan slide proyeksi untuk jadwal pelayanan
  ibadah (misalnya pemimpin lagu, pembicara khotbah, pembaca firman, dan diaken).
- **Foto:** Ditampilkan pada slide presentasi untuk mengenalkan pelayan ibadah, menyambut keluarga
  atau anggota baru, serta dokumentasi kegiatan pelayanan.
- **Materi Pengumuman:** Brosur/flyer jadwal kebaktian, persekutuan, dan kegiatan sosial gereja.

#### 2. Tempat dan Keamanan Penyimpanan Data
Seluruh data jemaat disimpan secara lokal pada server/komputer milik `[Nama Gereja]` sendiri. Data
ini **tidak pernah dikirimkan** kepada pihak pengembang perangkat lunak (Wira Delta Indonesia), pihak
ketiga, maupun layanan pelacakan cloud eksternal. Keamanan data sepenuhnya berada di bawah kendali
tim tata usaha / multimedia gereja kami.

#### 3. Perlindungan Foto Anak dan Remaja
Sesuai amanat UU Perlindungan Data Pribadi (UU No. 27/2022):
- Penayangan foto anak-anak dan remaja pada layar proyeksi wajib mendapatkan persetujuan terlebih
  dahulu dari orang tua atau wali yang sah.
- Anggota jemaat berhak meminta agar foto dirinya atau anaknya tidak ditayangkan di layar publik.
  Gereja akan menghormati permintaan ini dan menggantinya dengan tampilan teks.

#### 4. Masa Penyimpanan Data
Data jadwal ibadah dan berkas foto disimpan di sistem lokal selama `[misal: 1 tahun / selama
dibutuhkan untuk arsip pelayanan]`. Berkas yang sudah tidak relevan akan dihapus secara berkala dari
sistem dan media penyimpanan lokal.

#### 5. Hak Anggota Jemaat
Sebagai pemilik data pribadi, Anda memiliki hak untuk:
- Mengetahui informasi atau foto apa saja mengenai diri Anda yang tersimpan di sistem penampil.
- Meminta penghapusan atau penggantian foto Anda dari slide ibadah yang akan datang.
- Menarik persetujuan publikasi foto sewaktu-waktu.

Untuk menyampaikan permintaan terkait data pribadi Anda, silakan hubungi sekretariat gereja kami
melalui `[Kontak Sekretariat Gereja]`.
